---
title: "Fetch Disputes"
description: "Get the recovery disputes from your Chargeblast account—with win, loss, and submission outcomes."
---

```text
openapi: get /api/v2/disputes
```

<Info>
  Pass `managedByChargeblast=true` to return only the disputes Chargeblast fights on your behalf. Each dispute carries `disputeStatus` (`needs_response`, `under_review`, `won`, `lost`, ...), `isManagedByChargeblast`, `isSubmittedEvidenceByChargeblast` and `submissionDate`; fields that do not apply are omitted from the JSON rather than sent as `null`.
</Info>

<Note>
  This endpoint is enabled per account. Ask your account manager to turn it on for your API key. Use `startDate` / `endDate` (ISO 8601 or `yyyy-MM-dd`) for incremental pulls and keep `per` at 25 or below.
</Note>
